

Question: Main kaise pata kar sakta/sakti hoon ki koi specific scheme ke liye qualify karta hai ya nahi?

Response: Aap HD app mein yeh pata kar sakte hain. Aapko scheme par click karke eligibility criteria check karna hoga. In steps ko follow karein:
1. HD app kholein aur login karein.
2. Schemes section mein jaayein.
3. Jis specific scheme mein aap interested hain, use dhoondhein aur click karein.
4. Scheme details mein "Eligibility Criteria" ya isi tarah ka section dhoondhein.
5. Listed criteria ko review karein taki pata chale ki koi qualify karta hai ya nahi.
