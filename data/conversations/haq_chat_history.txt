User: When the citizen is eligible, certain schemes do not show in the eligible schemes. Why?

Response: If the questionnaire is completed correctly, the relevant schemes will be shown as per the eligibility criteria. To address this issue:

1. Ensure that the scheme you are looking for is mapped under your organization.
2. Double-check that all questionnaire answers are accurate and complete.
3. Verify that you have the latest app version and data update.
4. Check if the scheme is active and currently available in your region.

If all seems correct but schemes are still missing, try these steps:
a. Perform a data sync in the app.
b. Log out and log back into the app.
c. Clear the app cache and restart the app.

If the problem persists, report the issue to your supervisor or the technical support team, providing specific examples of missing schemes.
